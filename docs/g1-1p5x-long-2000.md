# G1 1.5x long-horizon 2000-step validation (2026-09)

## Status

**COMPLETE.** Decision: **PROMOTE_G1_1P5X_4000STEP**.

## Motivation

Stress-grid 1.5x showed G1 better than ungated Control at every matched
checkpoint through step 500 (ΔBalanced −0.03 to −0.14). Historical 1.0x
long-runs faded to a final tie by step 8000. This experiment asks whether the
1.5x advantage **persists through step 2000**, or is early-only.

## Trajectory metrics

```text
mean ΔBalanced (500…2000):     -0.0303
normalized area ΔBalanced:     -0.0308
```

**All seven checkpoints favor G1.** The advantage does **not** decay to zero
by 2000; it deepens around 1000–1250 (−0.04 to −0.046), dips at 1750
(−0.003), and remains −0.025 at 2000.

Classification: **strong persistence** (Case A), not early-only.

## Interpretation

> At 1.5x LR on seed1, G1's sample-efficiency advantage **persists through
> step 2000** (mean ΔBalanced ≈ −0.030 over 500–2000). This is **not** an
> early-only effect at this horizon.

Compared with historical 1.0x (advantage faded toward tie by 5000–8000),
1.5x has not yet shown that fade within 2000 steps.

Wording: *strong early and mid-horizon sample-efficiency; advantage still
established at 2000*. This is **not** yet a final-quality claim at 4000/8000.
